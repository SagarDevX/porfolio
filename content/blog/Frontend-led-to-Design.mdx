Like a lot of people start web development, I start with the basics, HTML, CSS, JS.

## It Started With Frontend
At first, I love how it was: write code, run localhost:3000, see something appear on screen. That feeling hooked me.

I start building things, breaking them, fixing them & understand how the system works. Then I moved to React & Next.js & got comfortable how ideas turn into interfaces.

Slowly by slowly, I was becoming more obsessed with the interfaces.

## Then I Started Noticing Something

 After develop some project, I realized that I wasn’t only building things I also cared how they look ,color , spacings and the way component felt after some little things like spaces added to it.

I spend a huge amount of time just to moving thing around by few pixels ,changing or selecting perfect colors ,adjust spacing, trying different fonts,tweaking animation & sweat the details until it feel right.

## But I Was Missing Something

While curiously search for things ,I found that the real problem was I don’t know any design related software like Figma, Adobe XD.I didn’t understand typography beyond picking a font that looks good. 

I wasn’t confident with color theory, grids & other fundamentals which are suppose to core of designer.

I felt like I had to choose a lane - go deeper in frontend or start learning design from scratch.

## Then I Found a Name for It

Design engineer - Frontend with design skills.

After then ,I came across the term “Design Engineer”.
Design engineer sits somewhere between the design & engineering. Someone who care about how component looks, how it behave & how it’s actually built.

## People Who helped Me Along the Way

I also didn’t find all of this by my own. Along the way, there were teachers ,coaches and coders who help me to find it.

Some taught me technical side & other other help me see interfaces differently.

[Manu Arora](https://www.youtube.com/@manuarora) & [Dive Club](https://www.youtube.com/@joindiveclub) are one of the Yt channel who help me understand how to improve interfaces & how important to sweat the details.

[Bro Code](https://www.youtube.com/@BroCodez) & other peoples help me with technical skills like how things built.


## What I’ve Learned So Far

I’m still very much at the beginning.
I’m still learning the fundamentals.

I’m continuing to improve my frontend skills while learning design alongside them.

If you’re in the same situation ,where you notice how visuals matter alongside functionality, then maybe you’re on the right path too.
